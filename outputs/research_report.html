11. Conclusion

This experimental study establishes that with rigorous de-duplication, strict train/test isolation, Min-Max normalization, and multicollinearity filtering, Random Forest provides robust, near-perfect intrusion classification on CSE-CIC-IDS2018 cloud traffic. Furthermore, SHAP-driven feature distillation reduces feature dimensionality by 78.9% (from 38 to 8 features) while retaining full detection capability and boosting throughput to 12,477 flows/sec.
